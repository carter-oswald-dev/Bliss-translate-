Why this exists

Blissymbols can say things very precisely, but most tools make you already know the grammar. For example, there are separate symbols for I (just one person) and we (a group that includes me), and a small mark that turns one thing into many. If a tool never asks, you can't tell it which one you meant.

Bliss Narrower works the other way round: it starts from every symbol your words could mean, then asks short, plain questions to narrow it down. The counter at the top shows how many different meanings are still possible, and it drops to 1 when everything is settled.

Good to know

  • Little words like a, an, the, will and did are usually left out. Bliss shows that information with marks on the other symbols instead.
  • Indicators (the small marks on top) are placed where the original symbol had one, or centred if it had none.
  • This is a helper, not an official translator. Check important messages with someone who knows Bliss well.
